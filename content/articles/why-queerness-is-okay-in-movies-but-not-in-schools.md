Has society become more accepting of queer people, or just more comfortable consuming queer stories? Those are not the same question, and treating them like they are is how you end up applauding a film at a festival and voting against a curriculum a month later.

This one's raw and unfinished on purpose — a set of observations from actually being in these rooms, not a polished thesis. Representation as entertainment has never been the same fight as representation as policy, and it's worth being honest about which one we're actually funding.
