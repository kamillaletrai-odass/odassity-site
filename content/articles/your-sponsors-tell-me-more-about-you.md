Every "about" page says roughly the same thing. Every sponsorship deal says something different — and something truer.

You are shaped as much by the opportunities you turn down as the ones you accept. The brand partnership you passed on because it felt off is a better biography than any bio you'd write about yourself.

This is an autopsy of that gap: what creators claim to stand for, versus what their actual choices — sponsors, collabs, the ads they let run — reveal about what they'll trade and what they won't.
