Your identity crisis isn't a personal failing. It's a predictable outcome of growing up inside systems — platforms, algorithms, onboarding flows — that were designed to tell you who you are before you'd finished deciding for yourself.

You were a person before you were a user. Somewhere between the sign-up form and the recommendation engine, one got mistaken for the other.

A mirror held up to the pipeline: profile prompts, personality quizzes, "for you" pages, and the quiet way each one asks you to describe yourself in terms a database can sort.
