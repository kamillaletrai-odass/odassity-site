We used to travel because the world was interesting. Somewhere along the way, the destination stopped being the point — the documentation became the trip.

It isn't that people have stopped loving Lisbon or Kyoto or the coastline outside Positano. It's that loving a place quietly, without an audience, has started to feel almost wasteful. If no one saw the sunset, did the identity update even happen?

> The itinerary is the pitch deck. The captions are the investor call.

This isn't a takedown of travel, or of posting. It's an invitation to notice the moment a trip stops being for you and starts being for the feed — and to ask what you actually wanted from the place before the algorithm got a vote.
